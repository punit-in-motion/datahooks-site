What We Do

We make your data work for your business

Data problems look technical, but they start with people. We align your teams, fix the foundation, and build dashboards and automations that work.

The Starting Point

We Get Your Teams Aligned

Before we touch a single record, we get everyone in the room. Across Sales, Marketing, Operations, Finance. Whoever owns or depends on your data. We discover where definitions break down, where data gets duplicated or lost, and where your teams disagree. Then we fix it together.

What that looks like in practice:

  • ✓ Align on what "customer" actually means to everyone
  • ✓ Map where data lives and who owns it
  • ✓ Find and fix the gaps: duplicates, contradictions, stale records
  • ✓ Build trust that the data is right
How we approach it
01
Discover what "right" looks like
Workshops with your teams. What metrics matter? What counts as a customer? Where do definitions clash?
02
Map and diagnose
We audit your sources and show you exactly where alignment breaks down. Read-only, no changes yet.
03
Fix and verify
Deduplicate, normalize, and validate. You sign off at every step. Build trust as we go.
04
Monitor and maintain
Quality checks catch drift before it becomes a problem. Your teams stay confident in the numbers.
Bringing It Together

Your Dashboards and Reports Finally Work

Once alignment is real, we connect every source into a single view. Your teams stop jumping between tools. Decisions get faster. Everyone trusts the same numbers. No more arguments about which number is right.

  • ✓ One place where all your data lives
  • ✓ Real-time dashboards everyone agrees on
  • ✓ Teams stop maintaining duplicates
  • ✓ Speed up from "how did we get that number?" to "here's the answer"
A recent client had
"Our Sales team had one definition of 'closed deal', Finance had another, and Ops was reporting something else entirely. We couldn't trust any dashboard. Every meeting started with arguing about what the number actually was."
After working with us
