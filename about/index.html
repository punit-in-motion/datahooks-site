About Us

We believe data should work for you

Datahooks was built on a simple idea: data problems look technical, but they're really people problems. Different teams have different definitions of what the data should do. We bridge that gap.

Our Mission

Make data a team sport

Your marketing team wants customer data yesterday. Finance needs accountability on every number. Sales can't trust what they see. And everyone's using different definitions of the same metric.

We bridge the gap between your teams and your data. We align everyone on what needs to happen, fix the data that's broken, and make sure the dashboards, automations, and AI your team is building work. You get a partner who stays accountable.

Our Promise
Trusted data.
Aligned teams.
A partner who stays.
