About Us

We believe data should work for you

Data Hooks was built on a simple idea: companies shouldn't need a 20-person data team to get their data right. They just need the right partner.

Our Mission

Democratize data infrastructure

Every company is becoming an AI company — but most aren't ready. Their data is scattered across dozens of tools, riddled with inconsistencies, and locked behind third-party walls.

We're changing that. Data Hooks gives every team — from startups to enterprises — the clean, unified, self-owned data infrastructure that used to require a dedicated platform engineering team and millions in budget.
