What We Do

We get your data where it needs to be

Most companies know their data is a problem. They just don't have the team to fix it. That's where we come in.

The Starting Point

Data Cleaning & Validation

This is usually where we begin. We audit your existing data sources, find the problems — duplicates, broken schemas, stale records, inconsistent formatting — and fix them. It's not glamorous, but it's the foundation everything else is built on.

What that looks like in practice:

  • ✓ Deduplication across millions of records
  • ✓ Schema validation and type enforcement
  • ✓ Address, email, and phone normalization
  • ✓ Ongoing data quality monitoring
How we approach it
01
Full audit before we touch anything
Read-only credentials. We map every source and flag every issue before making a single change.
02
Findings doc with a clear plan
Here's what's broken, here's what it'll take to fix it, here's our recommendation. Nothing moves without your sign-off.
03
Remediation in batches
Dedup, normalize, validate — methodically, with client approval at every gate. No shortcuts.
04
Ongoing monitoring
Clean data doesn't stay clean on its own. We set up quality checks that catch drift before it becomes a problem.
Bringing It Together

Data Unification

Once your data is clean, the next step is making it useful. We build pipelines from every source into a single, queryable layer — so your team stops jumping between dashboards and starts seeing the whole picture.

  • ✓ Connectors for every major platform (and your custom ones)
  • ✓ Identity resolution across platforms
  • ✓ Incremental syncing — only move what changed
  • ✓ Automated schema mapping and transformation
A recent client had
"We had customer data in Salesforce, product usage in Postgres, marketing in HubSpot, payments in Stripe, and support tickets in Intercom. Nobody could answer a simple question like which customers are at risk of churning."
After working with us
One query. All five sources. Real-time. Their team went from guessing to knowing — and they didn't have to learn a new tool.
The End Game

Your Data, Your Infrastructure

This is where it gets interesting. We help you move off the per-row, per-seat SaaS data tools and onto infrastructure you own. Your data lake. Your warehouse. Your queries. No more paying rent on your own data — and no more giving third parties a front-row seat to your business.

  • ✓ Warehouse setup on your cloud (AWS, GCP, Azure)
  • ✓ Transformation layer with version control
  • ✓ BI and analytics tooling
  • ✓ SOC 2 and GDPR compliant by default

We keep it simple. No bloated scoping exercises. Just a structured process designed to get it right the first time.

01

Discovery Call

02
